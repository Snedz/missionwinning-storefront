Best ATS resume templates (2026)

A practical checklist — not a bought “Top 10” list with fake stars. Updated 2026-09-17.

What “ATS-friendly” actually means

  • Single column; normal heading text (Experience, Education, Skills)
  • No critical content trapped in images or text boxes
  • Bullets tied to real evidence — never invent employers or metrics
  • File you can export to PDF/DOCX without exotic fonts

Template shapes that usually work

  1. Classic single-column — safest for parsers.
  2. Markdown → PDF — great for engineers who version-control content.
  3. Simple DOCX theme — easy for non-technical edits in Word/Google Docs.
