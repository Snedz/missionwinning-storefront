ATS keywords without the theater

Updated 2026-09-17 · A short workflow you can run in 20 minutes with the job-paste worksheet in the kit.

Direct answer: Pull 6–10 must-have terms from the posting, pair each with a real evidence bullet, keep a single-column layout, and skip invented employers/metrics.

Workflow

  1. Paste the posting into the worksheet. Highlight required vs nice-to-have.
  2. List must-haves (tools, domain nouns, outcomes). Cap at ~10.
  3. Map evidence — one bullet per must-have you actually did. Delete orphans.
  4. Mirror language lightly — use their noun if it’s accurate; don’t synonym-spam.
  5. Export plain — single column, standard headings, no text-in-images.
  6. Honesty gate — no invented employers, dates, or metrics.

What we refuse to claim

Guaranteed interviews, universal “ATS pass rates,” or that any score tool speaks for every employer’s parser.
