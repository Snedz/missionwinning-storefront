What is in the zip?

The ATS Resume Kit is a one-time set of practical files for shaping an honest, readable application package.

  • ATS-friendly resume templates and worksheets.
  • A fictional example resume to show structure and scannability.
  • A cover letter scaffold with prompts for your own evidence.
  • Honesty and export checklists for a final review.

Important: This is a template kit, not a job guarantee. It cannot promise interviews, offers, or employment; your experience, choices, and the market determine outcomes.
