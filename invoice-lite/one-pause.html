Pause new work

Use this plain notice when an invoice is late and you are pausing new work. Fill in the brackets before sending.

Hello [Client],

Invoice [Invoice] was due [Due date]. I am pausing new work until it is paid. Already delivered files stay yours.

This is not a lawsuit and not legal advice.

Sign [Your name]

Confirm your records and any existing agreement before sending. This page does not create a payment link or change your terms.
