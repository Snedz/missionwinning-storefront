Resume Kit

Salary talk checklist

Free companion to the pack. Use after you have a written offer.

  1. Get the offer in writing (base, bonus, equity, start date).
  2. Research range for title + city (levels.fyi, Glassdoor, peers).
  3. Ask one clear ask: base OR signing — not both at once unless strong leverage.
  4. Trade: if they cannot move base, ask for start date, title, or review at 6 months.
  5. Confirm everything in email before you resign.
