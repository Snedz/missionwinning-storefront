Gap year, without a fake job

Updated 2026-09-17 · For a year off, a layoff stretch, or any hole a reader will notice.

Direct answer: Put the dates in order and label the gap with something true. One line is enough. Do not invent an employer, title, or metric to fill the space.

A line you can say out loud

  • Study or a course — name the subject if you finished or seriously attended. Do not invent a certificate.
  • Caregiving or health — “Family caregiving” or “Medical leave” is enough. Details are optional.
  • Travel or a sabbatical — say so if you would say so in an interview. Add a project only if you did one.
  • Job search — acceptable. You can also leave the line short and explain in conversation.

Projects stay projects

Freelance, volunteer, or a personal build can sit under Projects with real dates. Do not restyle them as full-time employment. The honesty checklist in the kit is the gate: if you would not show the artifact, do not claim the bullet.

What we refuse to claim

That any phrasing “passes ATS,” hides a gap from humans, or is legal advice about what you must disclose.
