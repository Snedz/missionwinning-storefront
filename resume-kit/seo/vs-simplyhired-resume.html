ATS Resume Kit vs SimplyHired Resume

Updated 2026-09-17 · SimplyHired is presented here as a hosted job-search option. ATS Resume Kit is a one-time download with editable files. Confirm current plans, account, and export terms on SimplyHired before choosing.

Pick the kit if…

You want a compact local starting point, already have an editor, and want to tailor truthful evidence to a job posting.

Pick SimplyHired if…

You prefer a hosted job-search workflow. Read its live account, privacy, and export terms before paying or uploading sensitive career details.
