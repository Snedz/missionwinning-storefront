Before you send your resume

Take one quiet pass through this checklist before attaching your resume to an application or sending it to a person.

Send-ready checklist

  • Job title matches: the title on your resume is the role you are actually applying for, or is intentionally broad and accurate.
  • Keywords are true: every skill, tool, credential, and phrase matches your real experience. Do not add keywords just to chase a scanner.
  • One page or two on purpose: the length fits your experience and the target role; there is no accidental spillover or cramped filler.
  • PDF, not a screenshot: open the saved PDF and confirm the text can be selected, searched, and read in the correct order.
  • Follow-up date written: record when you sent it and the next date you plan to follow up, if a follow-up is appropriate.
