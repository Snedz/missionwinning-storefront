Cover letter length

Updated 2026-09-17 · A length guide for the cover scaffold in the kit. Not a hiring guarantee.

Direct answer: Default to one page unless the posting says otherwise. Open with the role, give two or three pieces of evidence you can defend, and stop. Do not pad to a magic word count.

A usable shape

  1. Open — role title, how you found it if that is true, and one sentence on why you are writing.
  2. Proof — two or three short paragraphs. Each one maps a posting must-have to work you actually did.
  3. Close — availability or next step you are willing to offer. No fake enthusiasm metrics.

What to cut

KeepCut
A claim the resume already supportsA second copy of every resume bullet
The posting’s own nouns, used once, accuratelySynonym stuffing to hit a word count
A gap you are willing to name in one lineA paragraph of apology or invented employment

What we refuse to claim

A universal 250- or 300-word law, “cover letters that get interviews,” or that a longer letter ranks higher in any ATS.
