Why not Google Docs for invoices?

Docs work until you need consistent numbering, a Print-PDF that doesn’t shift margins, and a pack you can send without rewriting the layout each time.

  • Stay on Docs if you invoice once a quarter.
  • Use Invoice Lite if you want a browser form → Print PDF with a price floor ($9 pack), not an agency retainer.

No invented “saved 10 hours/week” claims — just fewer broken layouts.
