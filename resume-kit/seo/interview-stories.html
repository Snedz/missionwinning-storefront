Interview Stories

A plain STAR skeleton for preparing honest, specific interview answers.

Situation

What was happening, where, and why did it matter? Name only facts you can support.

Notes: ________________________________

Task

What responsibility, goal, or constraint was yours?

Notes: ________________________________

Action

What did you personally do? List the decisions, steps, collaboration, and trade-offs in order.

Notes: ________________________________

Result

What changed? Include a measured result when one is available, and say what you learned when it is not.

Notes: ________________________________

Honesty check

  • Can I distinguish my contribution from the team's contribution?
  • Can I explain the evidence for each result?
  • Have I avoided inventing metrics, customers, or outcomes?
