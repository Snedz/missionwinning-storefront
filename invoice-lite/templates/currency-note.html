Currency note (manual FX)

Invoice Lite · owner-entered footnote for a foreign-currency invoice

Footnote wording (paste onto the invoice)

[reference] is billed as [invoice amount]. For [whose] records, that is approximately [manual figure] [note currency] at [manual rate] ([source + date]). Manual figure only — the billed amount in the invoice currency is what is owed.

Formula used (show your work): invoiced amount × your manual rate. No fees, spreads, or settlement differences are included unless you state them.

Rules

☐ The invoiced amount is stated ONLY in the invoice currency — the note never replaces it

☐ Rate, source, and date are filled in (a rate without a source is a guess; label guesses as guesses or leave the field blank)

☐ The client agreed which currency settles the bill — say that plainly if it was discussed
