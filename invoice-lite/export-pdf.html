Print the HTML invoice to PDF

Invoice Lite uses the browser print flow. Fill in the invoice preview, then save the print output as a PDF on your device.

Steps

  1. Open the Invoice Lite invoice builder.
  2. Enter your business, client, invoice number, line items, and any applicable tax details.
  3. Review the white invoice preview and remove any placeholder or fake sample values.
  4. Select Print / Save PDF, or press Ctrl+P on Windows/Linux or ⌘+P on macOS.
  5. In the browser print dialog, choose Save to PDF, confirm the page preview, and save the file.

Important

The PDF is created by your browser when you print. A generated PDF does not ship in the zip; the pack contains editable HTML templates and the browser-based workflow.

Check the saved PDF before sending it. Invoice Lite is a template and is not tax or legal advice.
