How to list an internship on a resume

Updated 2026-09-17 · Practical guidance for an accurate resume.

Short answer: Use true dates, titles, and evidence. This page does not promise ATS results, interviews, or a way to conceal facts.

Give the internship its own entry with the organization, location, dates, and the title you actually held. “Intern,” “co-op,” or the employer’s documented title is enough.

Show useful evidence

Choose two or three bullets about work you completed, tools you used, or deliverables you can discuss. Add a link only when it is safe to share and genuinely yours.

Do not overstate it

Do not claim ownership, metrics, or employment status you did not have. A small accurate entry is more defensible than a polished fiction.
