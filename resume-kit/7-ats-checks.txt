7 ATS checks (free) — before you send another application

1. One column for core experience. Two-column layouts reorder in many parsers.
2. Standard headings (Experience, Education, Skills). Custom headings can drop a whole section.
3. No critical text inside images or text boxes — parsers read text, not pictures.
4. Real employers and dates only. A gap reads better than a fabricated role.
5. Keywords only where you can defend them. Every claim is an interview question.
6. Export via Print -> Save as PDF, or a clean DOCX. Don't send a design export.
7. Filename: FirstLast_Role_Resume.pdf. Recruiters download dozens; make yours findable.

That's the whole list — no signup, no email capture.
