Returning to work after a career break

Updated 2026-09-17 · Practical guidance for an accurate resume.

Short answer: Use true dates, titles, and evidence. This page does not promise ATS results, interviews, or a way to conceal facts.

Put your real work history in order, then name the break at the level of detail you choose. “Family caregiving,” “Medical leave,” “Study,” or “Job search” can be enough; private details are optional.

Use current evidence

Lead with recent skills, projects, courses, volunteer work, or part-time work only when they are real. Label projects as projects rather than disguising them as jobs.

Keep the promise modest

No wording trick guarantees an interview or hides a gap from every reader. Aim for clean dates, readable structure, and claims you can explain calmly.
