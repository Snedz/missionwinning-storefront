A LinkedIn headline with signal

Say what you do, what you are good at, and where you are headed. Keep it specific enough for a person to understand in one glance.

  • Role: use your current or recent work, without inflating the title.
  • Strength: name one or two skills you can support with examples.
  • Direction: add the kind of work you are open to, if useful.

Example: Operations coordinator | Clear documentation and calm process cleanup | Open to customer operations.

Skip vague superlatives and keyword piles. A headline is a signpost, not a promise of an outcome.
