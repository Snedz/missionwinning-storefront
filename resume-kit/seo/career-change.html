Career-change resume

Updated 2026-09-17 · For people moving fields with evidence they already have.

Direct answer: Lead with a two-line summary of the move, then normal jobs in date order. Translate bullets toward the posting. Never award yourself the new title.

Workflow

  1. Paste the target posting. Mark must-haves you have actually done, even if the old job used different words.
  2. Write a summary: the direction you want, plus one proof line. Label a target title as a target.
  3. Keep employers, dates, and titles accurate. Rewrite bullets, not history.
  4. Add a short skills line only for tools you can demo or describe.
  5. Run the honesty gate before export. Single column, standard headings.

What we refuse to claim

That career changers “beat ATS” with a functional template, or that this kit places you in a new field. No outcomes, no buyer stories.
