Resume builder reviews & complaints (2026)

Direct-answer page for people searching review/complaint themes before buying another builder. No invented star ratings or fake customer quotes. Themes below are composite patterns from public discourse — verify any specific vendor on their site and your own card statement.

Direct answer: The loudest complaints about resume builders are rarely “the template looked ugly.” They are subscription surprise, ATS score theater, exports that break in portals, and pressure to invent fluff. If you only need a clean file this week, a one-time kit can be enough. If you want an ongoing editor, stay on SaaS — just go in eyes open.

Complaint themes (composite)

  • Subscription & billing friction — trial → paid without a clear “I only needed one PDF” path.
  • ATS score theater — a vendor-specific number that does not transfer to Workday/Greenhouse/etc.
  • Keyword stuffing nudges — prompts that push tools or metrics you cannot defend in interview.
  • Multi-column / design-heavy exports — look premium, parse poorly.
  • Account lock-in — hard to leave with a plain DOCX you fully own.

What we do differently (kit)

  • One-time zip — no monthly dashboard in v0.
  • Honesty checklist: do not invent employers, dates, or metrics.
  • Job-paste worksheet instead of magic scores.
  • Comparison pages so you can pick kit vs SaaS calmly.

What we will not claim

Guaranteed interviews, “98% ATS pass,” copied review counts, or invented MRR from social posts.
