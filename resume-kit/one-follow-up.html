One follow-up, then stop

Send one follow-up seven days after applying. Keep it brief, useful, and easy to answer. Then stop—no repeated nudges or pressure.

Four-line template

Hi [Name],
I applied for the [Role] role at [Company] on [date].
I’m following up once to reiterate my interest and ask whether there is an update.
Best, [Name]

This is a communication guideline, not a job guarantee. Hiring timelines and outcomes vary.
