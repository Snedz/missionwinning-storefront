Numbers you can defend

Only put a number on a resume if you can explain how it was measured in an interview. Keep the source, timeframe, baseline, and your contribution clear. If you cannot verify it, leave it out.

Fictional examples

These are examples only—not the founder’s results.

  • Example: Reduced average support-response time by 18% over one quarter by reorganizing the triage queue.
  • Example: Increased a fictional newsletter’s weekly opt-in rate from 4.2% to 5.1% during a six-week test.
  • Example: Cut a fictional team’s monthly reporting process from 6 hours to 2 hours by automating three recurring exports.

Use this as an honesty check, not a promise of interviews or employment. There is no job guarantee.
