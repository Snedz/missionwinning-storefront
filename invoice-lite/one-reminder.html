One polite payment reminder

A simple, one-time note for an invoice you have already issued.

Before sending: Verify the client, invoice number, due date, and your usual contact channel. Send this once, then stop. Do not add a fee, threat, or payment link that was not already agreed.

Hello [Client],

This is a friendly reminder that invoice [Invoice] was due on [Due date]. When convenient, please check your records and arrange payment using the payment method already agreed.

If you have already paid, please disregard this note. If anything is incorrect, reply through our usual verified channel so we can reconcile the records.

This is one polite reminder only; I will not send another reminder from this template.

Thank you,
[Your name]

This is not a collections threat and not legal advice. It is a plain reminder template, not a contract or demand.
