Invoice Lite

Rate floor

Publish a number you will not go under. Clients can see it. You can still charge more for rush or scope.

  • Hourly floor: write yours (example only: $75). Do not copy a number you have not chosen.
  • Day rate: floor × 6 to 8, not × 24.
  • Deposit: 30–50% before work starts on new clients.
  • Late: a written late fee or a pause. Say which one in the invoice.

This page does not set your price. It is the habit. See also the templates folder.
