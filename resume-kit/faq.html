Frequently asked questions

Is this a one-time purchase?

Yes. It is a one-time digital download, not a subscription.

Is it ATS-friendly?

It is built around clean, readable layouts and ATS-safe export guidance. Results vary by employer system.

What is the refund policy?

The checkout page is the refund surface. This page does not add a second policy.

Does it guarantee a job?

No. The kit cannot guarantee interviews, offers, or any job outcome.

Is the weekly vault required?

No. The weekly vault is optional; the core kit stands on its own. The sheet is the weekly theme. A missed day has no penalty, and there is no streak.

Where does Buy go?

Buy uses the checkout link in config.js. If that link is empty or not a real checkout, the button stays disabled on purpose.

Who is this for?

Roles are in gtm/ICP_ONE_PAGER.md. That page does not list buyers.
