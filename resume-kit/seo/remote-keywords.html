Remote keywords

Updated 2026-09-17 · Location language is just another must-have, not a spell.

Direct answer: Copy the posting’s term — remote, hybrid, distributed, async — only where your history supports it. Once is enough. Do not sprinkle it through every bullet.

Where a true word can sit

SpotUse it if
Location lineYou are actually able to work that way, and you want that constraint visible
Role lineThat job was remote or hybrid. Say which. Do not relabel an on-site job
One bulletYou can describe the overlap, the tool, or the shipped work without adding new metrics

Leave these out

“Remote remote remote,” a tools list you have not used, and any score that claims you are “100% remote-ready.” Timezone claims only if you will keep them.

What we refuse to claim

Interview rates for remote roles, or that keyword density changes an employer’s parser. Pair this page with the keyword worksheet, not with stuffing.
