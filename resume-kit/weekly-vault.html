Weekly vault

One week, not a daily streak. Miss a day and you still finish the week. No countdown, no shame counter.

This week (pick a Monday)

  1. Tailor 5 applications. Each one uses keywords from that job post.
  2. One human touch: a short note to someone who already works there, or a reply to a recruiter.
  3. One honest pass: cut any line you cannot defend in an interview.
  4. Log what you sent (company, role, date). Stop at the cap. Extra apps after the cap do not count as the weekly win.
