Frequently asked questions

Is Invoice Lite software?

No. It is a pack of editable templates, not software or a hosted billing app.

Is this tax advice?

No. The templates are not tax, legal, or accounting advice.

Is one template a W-9 form?

No. Invoice Lite does not provide a W-9 form.

Where does Buy go?

Buy uses the checkout link in config.js. If that link is empty or not a real checkout, the button stays disabled on purpose.

Are the ACME examples real?

No. Fictional ACME samples are fake examples for demonstration only.

Who is this for?

Roles are in gtm/ICP_ONE_PAGER.md. That page does not list clients.
