How to explain a layoff on a resume

Updated 2026-09-17 · Practical guidance for an accurate resume.

Short answer: Use true dates, titles, and evidence. This page does not promise ATS results, interviews, or a way to conceal facts.

A layoff is not a performance story you need to disguise. Keep the employer and dates accurate, then use one plain line if the context matters: “Role ended in a company-wide reduction.”

Keep the evidence real

Retain accomplishments you can support from the role. Do not change dates, rename the employer, or turn a job search into employment. You can share more context in a conversation if asked.

A simple structure

  • Company and real dates
  • Accurate title and scope
  • Optional one-line layoff context
  • Next role, project, or search activity only when true
