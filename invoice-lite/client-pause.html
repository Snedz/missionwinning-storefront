Client pause notice

Stub only. Work pauses if an invoice you already issued is unpaid past N days. This page is not a contract, not legal advice, not a termination, and not a collections letter. It does not know your balances.

Fill these in yourself

New work named in your note stops on that date. Files already delivered stay delivered. Resume when you record payment — or say a narrower condition you actually mean.

  1. Confirm the invoice is unpaid in your own records.
  2. Use N only if the client already agreed, or say that N is a new condition and wait.
  3. Copy the longer blanks in templates/client_pause_notice.md if you want a sendable note. Edit it. Do not attach this webpage.
