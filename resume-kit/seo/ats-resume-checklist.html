ATS resume checklist (2026)

Direct answer page for “ATS resume checklist.” Free to use. The paid kit packages these into editable files — get the kit, $29 one-time.

Direct answer: ATS-friendly resumes favor clean single-column structure, standard headings, real evidence tied to the posting, and zero invented employers/dates/metrics. Fancy multi-column designs and keyword stuffing theater often hurt more than they help.
  1. Layout: One column. Standard fonts. Clear section headings (Experience, Education, Skills).
  2. Contact: Name, email, phone, city/region, LinkedIn/portfolio if real.
  3. Evidence: Bullets with verbs + scope + outcome you can defend in an interview.
  4. Job-paste map: Copy 5–8 must-haves from the posting; place matching evidence beside each (worksheet in the kit).
  5. Honesty gate: Delete any employer, title, date, or metric you cannot source.
  6. Export: DOCX for most portals; PDF only when the listing asks. Spot-check that text is selectable.
  7. No magic scores: Ignore “98% ATS score” marketing. Different vendors parse differently.
