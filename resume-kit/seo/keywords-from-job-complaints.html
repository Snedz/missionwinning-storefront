Keywords from complaints, not keyword tools

App/SEO founders (and job seekers) often scrape volume tools. Better: read what humans complain about.

  1. Open 5 target job posts. Highlight required skills you actually have.
  2. Skim 1–2★ reviews for that company/role family — note repeated tool names and soft skills they punish.
  3. Put exact nouns on your Skills line. Bullets = verb + defended result.
  4. Export single-column PDF. Skip decorative columns.

Resume Kit’s checklist encodes this loop. We don’t invent employers to match a keyword list.
