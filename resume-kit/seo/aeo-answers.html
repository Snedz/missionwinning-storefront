ATS questions, answered honestly

Three questions people actually ask. Short answers, no invented stats, no fake review quotes.

Does a multi-column resume get rejected by the ATS?

Not "rejected" — mis-read, which is worse because it's silent. Most parsers extract text in document order; two columns often interleave, and anything in headers, footers, text boxes, or images can drop out entirely. A single-column layout with standard headings ("Experience", "Education") is the boring format that survives every vendor. You don't need a plain-looking resume, just one the parser can read in order.

Do I need to match every keyword in the posting?

No — and stuffing all of them hurts you twice: the human reading it sees robot filler, and you'll be asked about tools you can't discuss. Map the 5–8 must-haves, keep the ones you can back with a real project, and rewrite your bullets in the posting's vocabulary where it's genuinely accurate. Leave the rest off rather than bluff. Nobody can honestly promise a "keyword score" threshold, because vendors don't publish one.

Is a one-time resume kit enough, or do I need a subscription builder?

Depends on what you're missing. If your problem is structure and targeting — single-column files, keywords from the posting, honest bullets — a one-time kit plus a PDF export covers it, and you keep the files. If you want a hosted editor, tracking dashboard, and automatic formatting across many applications, a subscription tool earns its fee. The kit is the cheaper side of that fork, not a replacement for every reason someone subscribes.
