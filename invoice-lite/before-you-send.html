Before you send your invoice

Use this quick review to catch the small details that can delay payment or create confusion.

Send-ready checklist

  • Client name: use the client or business name and contact details that belong on this invoice.
  • Invoice number: assign a clear, unique invoice number and check it against your own records.
  • Due date: confirm the due date matches the agreed terms and is easy to find.
  • Deposit if any: show any deposit already paid or requested, and make the remaining balance clear.
  • No tax advice claim: verify the figures for your situation, but do not describe this template as tax advice or legal advice.
