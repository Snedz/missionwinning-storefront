Kill-fee note (cancellation)

Suggested wording

Hello [client],

Confirming the cancellation of [project / invoice reference]. Per the written terms we both agreed to — [quoted cancellation term] — a cancellation (kill) fee of [verified amount] applies. A separate invoice will reference this note.

Work already delivered to date is itemized in the project record; nothing further will be started. If any detail above differs from your records, please reply through our verified channel so we can reconcile.

Thank you,
[sender]

Internal checklist

☐ Written cancellation terms existed BEFORE work began (quoted exactly above)

☐ Amount recalculated from those terms and verified against your records

☐ Delivered-work record attached or referenced — no invented line items

☐ No threats, no invented late fees, no legal language you can't support

Template only. Not legal, tax, or collection advice. If terms were never agreed in writing, stop and talk to the client instead.
