What is in the zip?

The Invoice Lite download is a small, one-time template pack for solo operators who need a clean starting point for billing.

  • Invoice, receipt, statement, reminder, credit-note, rate-card, and thank-you templates.
  • Pricing and fulfillment notes for adapting the files to your own workflow.
  • One clearly fictional EXAMPLE invoice for demonstrating the layout.

Important: Invoice Lite is not accounting software, tax advice, payment processing, or a bookkeeping system. Check every amount and requirement yourself.
