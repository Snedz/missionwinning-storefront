References page

Updated 2026-09-17 · A separate page, not a block at the bottom of the resume.

Direct answer: Do not put references on the resume by default. Build a one-page list and send it when asked. Every person on it has already agreed.

Before you add a name

  1. Ask. A yes from last year is stale if the relationship changed.
  2. Write how they know the work in one line (manager, client, teammate) — the real relationship, not a boosted title.
  3. Use the contact channel they gave you. Do not guess an email.
  4. Keep the same name spelling and dates you used on the resume.

Layout that stays ATS-plain

Single column. Heading “References.” Three to five entries is enough if that is how many people agreed. Phone and email on their own lines. No photos, icons, or text inside shapes.

What we refuse to claim

That listing references improves a parser score, or that “references available upon request” is required. We do not supply sample people.
