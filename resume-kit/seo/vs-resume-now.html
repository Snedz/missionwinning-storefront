ATS Resume Kit vs Resume Now

Updated 2026-09-17 · Resume Now is presented here as a hosted resume-building option. ATS Resume Kit is a one-time download with editable files. Confirm current plans, pricing, account, and export terms on Resume Now before choosing.

ATS Resume KitResume Now
DeliveryDownloadable files edited locallyHosted resume-builder workflow; verify current access and exports
Included focusResume starter, job-paste worksheet, cover scaffold, ATS hygiene, and export checksGuided online resume creation; consult the live product for current scope
Account dependencyNot required after downloadDepends on the provider's current account and plan terms
Best fitYou prefer a portable, low-tech workflow and control of your filesYou prefer guided in-browser editing and are comfortable checking hosted-service terms
Honesty noteNo invented pricing, user counts, testimonials, buyer names, MRR, ATS scores, or hiring guarantees are used here.

Pick the kit if…

You want a compact local starting point, already have an editor, and want to tailor truthful evidence to a job posting.

Pick Resume Now if…

You prefer a hosted guided builder. Read its live pricing, renewal, privacy, and export terms before paying or uploading sensitive career details.
