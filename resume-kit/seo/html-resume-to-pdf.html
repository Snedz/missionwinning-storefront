HTML resume → PDF without breaking ATS

  1. Open the template HTML in Chrome.
  2. Cmd/Ctrl+P → Destination: Save as PDF.
  3. Margins: Default · Background graphics: off.
  4. Disable headers/footers (URLs in the margin look unprofessional and confuse some parsers).
  5. Open the PDF and confirm selectable text (not a screenshot).

Resume Kit templates are single-column on purpose. Multi-column Canva exports are the usual ATS failure mode.
