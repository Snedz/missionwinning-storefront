Scope-change note

Invoice Lite · neutral note before a change order

Suggested wording

Hi [client],

Thanks for the new ask — [new request]. That sits outside the original scope for [project / invoice reference], so before starting I want to keep the agreement clean:

1. Add it as a change order ([rough estimate, if given]) — I'll send the change-order template for written sign-off, or
2. Park it for a follow-up phase after the current deliverable.

Reply with 1 or 2 and I'll take it from there. Nothing outside the original scope starts until it's in writing.

— [sender]

Internal checklist

☐ Original scope document exists and was re-read before sending this note

☐ The new request is described in the client's own words — no exaggeration

☐ Any estimate is labeled rough; no firm price before the change order

☐ Tone is neutral: this protects both sides, not a complaint

Template only. Not a contract, invoice, or legal advice. Price and schedule changes are only real once both parties sign the change order.
