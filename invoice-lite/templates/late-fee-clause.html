Late-fee clause (optional)

Invoice Lite · neutral snippet for an agreed proposal or invoice

Suggested wording — use only if already agreed in writing

Amounts unpaid after [grace period] past the due date on [reference] may incur the fee stated in our signed agreement ([quoted fee]), subject to a cap of [cap] and to applicable law. This clause creates no new obligation: it restates terms both parties already signed.

Fees are never backdated, never compounded unless the signed terms say so, and never asserted where the agreement is silent. Disputes go through the approved channel on the invoice.
